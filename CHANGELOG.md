# W'orc out changelog

## 1.0.3
- Fixed a Lua error spam when entering combat (protected combat values). Distance pauses during combat, and kills are rechecked when combat ends.

## 1.0.2
- **Dance on the tracker**: after each finished exercise, your coach dances over the tracker window for 10 seconds.
- **Pause in dungeons** (Settings > General, "In dungeons too"): workouts wait until you leave dungeons and raids; triggers keep counting.
- **Distance**: only distance you move yourself counts. Flight paths, boats and zeppelins are now reliably ignored.
- French: shorter texts so nothing is cut off in the settings.

## 1.0.1
- **French translation**: everything switches to French on a French game client (settings, workout window, exercise names, group and guild messages).
- **Countdown**: after clicking Start, a big 3, 2, 1, GO! appears before the exercise (reps and timed).
- **Victory dances**: on success, your coach performs one of 6 dances at random (Macarena, YMCA, Twist, House, Snake and Step hip hop).
- **Tracker window**: a small see-through window shows your progress towards the next workouts (kills, distance, quests, time played). Move it, lock it or hide it in Settings > General.
- **Much lighter download**: animations now use WoW's compressed texture format (about 4 times smaller).

## 1.0.0
- First release.
- **Triggers**: level up, mobs killed, bosses, distance run, entering a capital, quests, deaths and time played, each with its own frequency and multiplier.
- **Workout window** with an animated coach (orc for the Horde, night elf for the Alliance), reps or a timer, and Done / Snooze / Skip.
- **Social**: offer to tell your group you're taking a short break, and share finished exercises with your guild.
- **Settings** (`/wo`): triggers, exercises (add your own), social, difficulty, coach, and stats with a day streak.
